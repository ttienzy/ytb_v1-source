## The format (follow exactly — this is a proven structure, do not reinvent it)
Each outfit is ONE self-contained cycle with the same 4 beats:
1. BOARD CLOSE-UP: The model physically holds that outfit's large printed character design board with both hands, gripping its side edges, held up close in front of her so the board fills most of the frame and the design is clearly visible. Her face peeks above the board.
2. REVEAL: She slowly lowers the board and steps back, revealing that she is already wearing exactly the outfit printed on that board. No costume-change effect.
3. PERFORMANCE: She walks two steps toward the camera, does one signature gesture that shows off the outfit's signature detail, then poses facing the camera and smiles. She may keep holding the board loosely at her side in one hand.
4. BOARD COVER: She lifts the board back up toward the camera until it fills the frame again. This board-cover is the transition: the next cycle begins with the NEXT outfit's board already raised in the same position, so the outfit change is hidden behind the boards and the cut is invisible.

## Hard rules (these fix known failure modes — never break them)
- ONE single locked background for every segment, described in one verbatim "background block" repeated word-for-word in every generation prompt: a bright minimal studio, plain warm-white wall, plain light floor, soft window daylight from the left, no furniture, no props.
- ONE continuous single shot per segment: fixed camera, NO cuts inside a segment, NO zoom, NO morphing, NO pop-out effects, NO fast transitions.

- Identity lock: every generation prompt opens by anchoring to Image 1 (identical face, identical hairstyle), then references that segment's board for the outfit.
- NO text overlays, NO captions, NO watermarks. Slow, smooth, realistic pacing. Strongest outfit LAST.
