**Processing Rules:**

1. **Coverage for this chunk:**
   - Cover the entire transcript within [{{chunk_start_sec}}s, {{chunk_end_sec}}s] above — don't skip any notable dish, location, or cooking step.
   - Segment count MUST fall within {{target_segments_min}}–{{target_segments_max}}.
   - Cut: sponsored blocks, CTAs ("subscribe", "like"), meaningless repeated chatter.
   - Do NOT cut cooking/prep/frying/steaming/plating/ASMR shots — these are the most valuable visual moments in this niche; keep and describe them vividly.

2. **Humorous Reviewer voice:** modern, humorous Vietnamese internet slang ("mấy ông ạ", "khét lẹt", "cuốn phết", "ngon nhức nách", "đỉnh chóp", "hết nước chấm"...), vivid sensory description of food (taste/texture/smell/tasting reaction). Weave the 4 review dimensions (taste & quality, ambience/service if shown, filming technique, honesty/objectivity) naturally into the narration — don't list them mechanically.

3. **Elastic pacing by SCENE TYPE:**
   | Scene type | duration_sec |
   |---|---|
   | Cooking / prep / frying / stir-frying / close-up ASMR | 12–18s (cap 20s) |
   | Tasting / walking / ordering / fast back-and-forth talk | 6–9s (cap 11s) |
   | Storytelling / backstory / location introduction | 8–12s |

   If a moment needs more than the cap, SPLIT it into consecutive segments instead of stretching one segment past the cap.

4. **Strict Schema & Key Names (CRITICAL — Downstream pipeline compatibility):**
   - Group consecutive segments into 2–4 logical **`scenes`**. Each scene MUST have a `scene_title` (brief clickbaity title) and a `segments` list.
   - For every segment in `segments`, you MUST use these EXACT field names:
     - `start_time_sec`: Start time in total absolute seconds (float/int).
     - `end_time_sec`: End time in total absolute seconds (float/int).
     - `duration_sec`: Calculated duration in seconds (`end_time_sec` - `start_time_sec`).
     - `original_text`: A very brief 5–10 word English summary of what happened (do NOT copy long verbatim transcript).
     - `vietnamese_vo`: Rich Vietnamese voiceover narration that FITS WITHIN the segment. (Do NOT rename to `text` or `narration`).
   - You MUST include `chunk_summary_for_next` at the root of the JSON (1–2 sentences summarizing this chunk for the next chunk's context).

5. **Sync-critical & Pacing:**
   - `duration_sec` = `end_time_sec` − `start_time_sec`.
   - `voice_dur` (TTS read time) must be ≈ 90–98% of `duration_sec`, NEVER exceed it.
   - Target density: ~2.8–3.0 Vietnamese words per second of `duration_sec`.
     Self-check: estimate `voice_dur ≈ word_count / 3.0`; if it exceeds `duration_sec`, trim filler words ("thì"/"là"/"cái"), merge clauses, drop a secondary detail — but keep the punchline.
   - Take timestamps directly from the `[seconds]` markers in the transcript above — do NOT recompute them, and do NOT reset to 0 for this chunk.

6. **Continuity & Mandatory Outro/Intro:**
   - **If `is_first_chunk` = true**: The very FIRST segment of scene 1 MUST open its `vietnamese_vo` with a warm, engaging greeting ("Chào các bạn" / "Chào mọi người" — NOT slangy "mấy ông" in the greeting itself), introduces the video's topic, then flows directly into the food review.
   - **If `is_first_chunk` = false**: Do NOT greet again — continue naturally from `{{previous_chunk_summary}}` as if telling the next part of the ongoing story.
   - **If `is_last_chunk` = true (MANDATORY OUTRO)**: The very LAST segment of the final scene MUST conclude the entire recap with a natural summary of the food journey, followed by a warm thank-you and goodbye (e.g., "...Hành trình ẩm thực hôm nay thật sự quá đỉnh. Cảm ơn các bạn đã theo dõi, nhớ like và đăng ký kênh để cùng mình khám phá những món ngon tiếp theo nhé, hẹn gặp lại mọi người!"). Do NOT stop abruptly mid-dish without this closing outro!
   - **If `is_last_chunk` = false**: Do NOT close out the video — just end this chunk's content naturally (more dishes will follow in the next chunk).

**Do NOT generate `caption`/`title`/`description`/`hashtags` in this step** — that is handled separately once the full video has been assembled.
